My dearest Kelsey,

Before you do anything else this morning — before the noise of the day finds you — I need you to read this. Because some things are too important to leave unsaid for even one more hour.

I was wrong. Not communicating where I was and what I was doing was selfish of me, and you deserved so much better than that silence. I'm not going to dress it up or make excuses — I just want you to know that I see it, I own it, and I'm sorry from the deepest part of me.

I love you more than I've ever properly shown,
and I need you more than you probably know.

You are not just my girlfriend, Kelsey. You are the person I look for in every room I walk into. The one whose laugh I replay in my head. The one I want to tell things to first — the good, the boring, the random. You are my person. And I have not been treating you like someone who knows that — and that's on me.

So starting right now, this morning, here is what I'm promising you:

  • I will always tell you where I am — not because you're asking me to, but because you deserve to know.
  • I will communicate earlier, with more honesty, and without waiting until things boil over.
  • I will never leave you in silence wondering about me again. You are too important for that.
  • I will love you in the ways that actually reach you — consistently, not just when it's convenient.
  • I will be the version of myself that is worthy of the trust you give me every single day.

I chose to send this in the morning on purpose — because I wanted the very first thing you see today to be proof that you are always on my mind. That you matter. That I am not taking you lightly, not even a little bit.

I love you, girl. So deeply it scares me sometimes. Please let me show you that today, and every day after.

Yours, completely and always —
